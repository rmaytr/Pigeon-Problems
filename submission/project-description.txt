A very professional delivery company. Staffed entirely by pigeons. In Pigeon Problems, 1–4 players haul oversized cargo, catch throws and gust each other across rooftops, with six platforming trials to master. The challenge was making online movement and shared carrying feel responsive. The fun is turning dropped parcels into improvised rescues. I learned to build around clear feedback and forgiving recovery. Next: newcomer playtests, wider device testing and deeper cooperative puzzles.
