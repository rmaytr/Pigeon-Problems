## Quick judge walkthrough

1. Choose **Solo practice → Delivery practice** to learn movement, gripping,
   throwing and landing. WASD/arrows move, Space hops/holds to flap, E grips or
   gently places cargo. Mouse aim turns the head within its normal range.
2. Try **Solo practice → Chimney Hop** for the first rooftop trial. Collect all
   three stamps, land to refill wing power, and reach the finish flag. Later
   courses add moving decks, updrafts, timed vane bridges and a freight lift.
3. For the central cooperative experience, choose **Clock in with friends →
   Create private crew**, copy the invite, and have one to three friends join on
   separate devices. Select a course or the classic five-delivery shift.
4. A second carrier steadies long or bulky cargo; helpers can catch throws, open
   routes and gust airborne teammates. All players share server-owned progress.
5. Use **Menu** for checkpoint recovery. Completed deliveries and collected
   stamps survive retries. Voice is optional; pings need no microphone.
